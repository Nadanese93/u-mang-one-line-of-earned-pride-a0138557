A Make.fun bounty entry for **One Line of Earned Pride** — one line, 1–240 characters, giving the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride.

The entry is a beekeeper's first honey frame: three summers of stings behind him, and this morning he lifts a frame heavy with honey up to the light while the bees go on working like he was one of them. A long effort finally landing, in one read.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes the line to `entry.txt` — 169 characters, no trailing newline.
